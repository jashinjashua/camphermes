Camp Levels

Meet the minimums for your level, then apply. Smoking, alcohol, drugs, chips, and soda are prohibited.

For each camp level, there are specific physical exercises and minimum repetitions you must be able to complete before applying.

Make sure you can complete the required numbers for the level you're interested in. Then click Apply and contact our team.
